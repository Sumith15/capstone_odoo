   Show a collection of product boxes alongside a descending sales performance chart. Communicate declining demand and products losing business momentum.

3. REVENUE CONCENTRATION RISK:

4. FRAGMENTED DATA & LACK OF TRUST:
   Show disconnected data sources, spreadsheets, database symbols and broken data connections converging into inconsistent reports. Communicate poor data quality and fragmented business information.
